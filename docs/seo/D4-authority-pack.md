**Post 2 — 实用清单（建议 3 天后，可长期带来流量）**

```
Five things to check before the container is sealed.

1. Carton count against the packing list — not the invoice. They disagree more often than you would expect.
2. Shipping marks. If the cartons are unmarked, nobody at the destination can tell one buyer's goods from another's.
3. The seal number, photographed while the door is still open.
4. Mixed SKUs in one container. Count each SKU separately; a total that matches can still hide a swap.
5. Damage that happened before loading. Once the door closes, that argument is unwinnable.

We supervise loading at Nansha, Shekou, Ningbo and Tianjin. The report is issued the same day, with photographs of the seal and the stow.

Full checklist and sample report: muzhuoinspection.com/sample-report
```

**Post 3 — 案例（建议 1 周后；数字取自已交付的 SIA 装柜报告，已脱敏）**

```
A recent loading supervision job, in numbers:

· 415 cartons
· 31 SKUs in one 45G1 container
· 13,913 pieces counted against the packing list
· 18 SKUs photographed in detail at the buyer's request
· One report issued the same evening

Mixed-SKU containers are where most shortages happen. When 31 product lines go into one box, a carton count alone proves very little — each line has to be counted and photographed as it goes in.

That is the difference between "the container was loaded" and "the container was loaded with what you paid for".

muzhuoinspection.com
```

## 2. 免费目录清单（5 个主选 + 3 个备选）

| 1 | Google Business Profile | https://business.google.com/create | 免费 | Google 账号、地址或服务区域、电话、验证（明信片/电话/视频） | 即时创建，验证 1–14 天 | 最高。影响品牌搜索、Google 地图、知识面板 |
| 2 | Crunchbase | https://www.crunchbase.com/register | 免费 | 注册账号、公司名、域名邮箱、简介、logo、成立年份 | 1–3 天 | 高权重域名，B2B 采购常查 |
| 3 | Trustpilot | https://business.trustpilot.com/signup | 免费版 | 域名验证（DNS 或邮箱）、公司信息 | 即时 | 评价页可被搜索收录，提升转化信任 |
